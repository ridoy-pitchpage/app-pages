Pen Clash

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Pen Clash is a game for iPhone and iPad: two pens on a school desk, and you flick yours to knock the other one off. It is built so that nothing about you leaves your device: Pen Clash does not collect any data.

What stays on your device

Your progress (the pens in your pen case, the classmates you have beaten and their stars, your daily medals), the names typed in for 2 Players, the pen you used last and the sound setting are saved only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • No network requests: the game works fully offline.
  • No in-app purchases.

Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

Pen Clash is a game for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included, shows no advertising and offers no purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Because Pen Clash collects no data, a change could never apply to data already collected.
