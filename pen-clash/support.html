Pen Clash

Support

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Pen Clash is the old school pen fight in 3D. Two pens on a desk: take turns flicking yours, knock theirs off, keep yours on. First to two rounds wins.

Questions

How do I flick?
On your turn, touch your glowing pen, pull back and let go. The arrow and the power meter show the shot; move your finger back onto the pen to cancel it. Touch the middle of the pen to push it straight, or an end to spin it.

How do I win more pens?
Play School Day. Each of the eight classmates has a pen; beat them and it joins your pen case.

How do two people play on one device?
Choose 2 Players on the home screen. On an iPad, lay it flat on the table and sit at either end: each player's card faces them.

What is the Daily Shot?
A fresh desk every day. Clear it in as few shots as you can: one shot is gold, two silver, three bronze.

How do I turn the sound off?
Tap the speaker at the top of the home screen.

Does it need the internet?
No. Everything works offline and nothing is sent anywhere.

Are there ads or purchases?
No. Pens are won by playing and can't be bought.

Will a new phone keep my progress?
Progress is part of the app's data, so an iCloud or computer backup of the device restores it.

How do I start again?
Delete the app and install it again. This erases all progress.
