Common questions

How do I play?
Tap a tile to turn its cable piece a quarter turn. When every piece joins up to the power station with no loose ends, every house has power and the town lights up.

How do I earn three stars?
Light the town in as few taps as possible. The play screen shows the best possible number of taps for each level.

What are coins and hints for?
You start with three free hints; a hint turns one piece the right way. Coins come from new stars and from the daily town, and buy more hints when the free ones are used. There is nothing to buy with real money.

What is the daily town?
A new town each day, the same for everyone. Light it on days in a row to grow your streak.

Can I replay a level?
Yes: tap Levels on the home screen and choose any level you have reached.

Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device, every time you finish a town. It is included in your iPhone or iPad backup, so restoring a backup brings it back. Deleting the app deletes it.

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Gridlight works fully offline and has no account, ads or tracking.

How do I turn the sound off?
Tap the speaker button at the top of the home screen.
