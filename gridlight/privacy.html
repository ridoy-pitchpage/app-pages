Gridlight

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Gridlight is a small puzzle game: turn the cable pieces until every house in a little town has power. Gridlight does not collect any data about you.

What stays on your device

Your progress (the level you have reached, your stars, coins and hints, the daily town streak) and the sound setting are saved only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and the game makes no network requests while you play.

Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Gridlight has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned by playing and can only be spent on hints inside the game.

Children

Gridlight is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included, since it collects nothing at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Because Gridlight collects no data, a change could never apply to data already collected.
