Doodle Reef

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Doodle Reef is a drawing game for children: you draw a sea creature and it swims in a reef of its own. It is built so that nothing about the child or the family leaves the device: Doodle Reef does not collect any data.

What stays on the device

Drawings, the reef, progress (pearls, stars, levels) and settings (sound, touch feedback) are saved only on the iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics, no third-party code that collects data, and no tracking of any kind. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Deleting the app deletes this data. A grown-up can also erase it at any time: Settings (the gear on the home screen) → the lock → answer the sum → press and hold Start over.

Sharing a creature

A grown-up can share a creature as a picture card, or a photo of the reef, through the standard iOS share sheet; the app asks the grown-ups' question first. The card holds the drawing in a code that Doodle Reef on another device can read. You choose where it goes (for example Messages, AirDrop or Photos); we never receive it. Saving to Photos asks for permission to add images only, never to read your library.

When a creature arrives from someone else (by its code, a link or a .reef file), the app reads only the drawing and adds it to the reef.

Children

Doodle Reef is made for children and is designed to meet Apple's Kids category rules and children's privacy laws such as COPPA and the GDPR: it collects no personal information from anyone, shows no advertising, offers no purchases, and keeps rating the app, writing to us and the share sheet behind a parental gate.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Because Doodle Reef collects no data, a change could never apply to data already collected.
