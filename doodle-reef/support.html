Common questions

How do I draw a creature?
Tap the yellow pencil, choose a creature (or a blank page), colour it in and tap the tick. It peels off the page and swims into the reef.

Where are the grown-up settings?
On the home screen, tap the gear, then the lock, and answer the sum. Behind it you can write to us or start over.

How do I send a creature to a friend?
In the reef, tap the creature, then the share button, and answer the grown-ups' sum. The picture card has a code in it: scan it with the Camera on a device with Doodle Reef and the creature swims into that reef.

How do I save a photo of the reef?
Tap the camera in the reef, then share and choose Save Image. iOS asks once for permission to add photos.

Does it need the internet?
No. Everything works offline and nothing is sent anywhere.

Are there ads or purchases?
No. Pearls are earned by playing and can't be bought.

Will a new phone keep our reef?
The reef is part of the app's data, so an iCloud or computer backup of the device restores it.

How do we start again?
Settings → the lock → answer the sum → press and hold the bin. This erases every drawing and all progress.
