Frost Rush

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Frost Rush is a runner-shooter game for iPhone and iPad. Frost Rush does not collect any data about you.

What stays on your device

Your progress (the level you have reached, coins, upgrades, stars, the difficulty you chose and whether sound is on) is saved only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and the game works without an internet connection.

Deleting the app deletes this progress.

Children

Frost Rush is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, since it collects nothing at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Because Frost Rush collects no data, a change could never apply to data already collected.
