Frost Rush

Support

Run the snowy bridge, blast the charging army, and beat the boss at the end.

How to play

  • Tap Start run. Your ranger fires on their own.
  • Drag left and right anywhere on the screen to steer.
  • Pass through a gate to take its gun or boost. Each gate shows how its gun compares to yours.
  • Dodge glowing shots, striped charge lanes, slam circles and ice waves. Beat the boss at the end.

Common questions

How do I change the difficulty or replay a level?
Tap the level card on the home screen, then choose Easy, Normal or Hard and any level you have unlocked. Stars are kept for each difficulty.

Do I keep coins if I lose?
Yes. Coins you pick up are kept after a defeat, a restart or leaving a run, so there is always progress. Spend them on firepower, fire rate and health on the home screen.

What happens after level 24?
Clearing the summit opens endless runs.

How do I pause or turn the sound off?
Tap the pause button during a run. Switching to another app pauses the run for you.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?
No. Frost Rush plays fully offline and has no accounts, ads or in-app purchases.

How do I start again from the beginning?
Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app starts a new game.
