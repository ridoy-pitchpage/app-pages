Fireflies

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

In short: Fireflies has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. If you just play, everything stays on your device. Only if you choose to sign in does the game keep a small amount of data online, so it can back up your progress and show your friends how you're doing. You can delete it from inside the app at any time.

Playing without an account

You never need an account to play. Your progress, settings, stars, collected fireflies and daily results are saved on your device, in the app's own storage. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

The optional account

If you sign in (Account, then "Email me a code"), you get a 6-digit code by email. Once you're signed in, Fireflies keeps:

  • Your email address, to send sign-in codes and recognise your account.
  • An account ID, a random identifier created at sign-in.
  • A name you choose (up to 20 characters, "Firefly" until you change it) and a friend code.
  • A backup of your progress: levels solved, stars, best times, collected fireflies and colours, hearts, boosters, daily puzzle results, statistics and your sound and vibration settings. Puzzles you're in the middle of stay on the device.
  • Your daily puzzle results: the time and number of hints for each daily puzzle you solve.
  • Your friends list: the players you've added by their friend code.

This is used only to run these features: backing up your game, restoring it on another device, and the Friends screen. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone except the service that stores it.

What your friends see

Only people who have added your friend code, or whose code you have added, can see you. They see your name, your journey level, your stars, and your time and hints for today's daily puzzle. They never see your email address. You can remove a friend at any time on the Friends screen, which removes the link for both of you.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the database and sends the sign-in emails, on servers in the United States. Supabase processes it only on our behalf. The connection is encrypted.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. To delete it, open Fireflies, tap the account button on the home screen, then Delete account. This removes your name, friend code, backup, daily results and friendships straight away. Progress on your device stays, and you can keep playing. You can also email us to ask for deletion or for a copy of your data.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Fireflies hands a short line of text (for example your daily result or your friend code) to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes; we don't see it.

Children

Fireflies is a gentle puzzle game anyone can play without an account. The optional account is not meant for children under 13: if you're under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), please play without signing in, or ask a parent first. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
