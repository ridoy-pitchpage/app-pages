How to play

  • Tap a dark square to place a firefly. Its light shines along the row and column until it meets a wall.
  • Two fireflies must never shine on each other.
  • A number on a wall says how many fireflies touch it (above, below, left and right). A 0 means none.
  • Light every square to solve the puzzle. Tap a square again to mark it with a dot ("no firefly here"), and once more to clear it.
  • The tutorial is always there: the question mark on the home screen, or Settings, then How to play.

Common questions

What are hearts and tries? Each journey level gives 3 tries; a firefly that breaks a rule costs one. Running out of tries uses a heart. Hearts come back by themselves, one every 30 minutes, and the daily puzzle is always free. There is nothing to buy.

Do I need an account? No. Everything works without one, offline. Signing in with your email is optional and lets you back up your progress, play on another device and add friends.

How do I move my progress to a new phone? Sign in on the old phone (the account button on the home screen), wait for "Progress backed up", then sign in with the same email on the new phone.

How do I add a friend? Open Friends, share your friend code, or type in theirs and tap Add.

How do I delete my account? Tap the account button on the home screen, then Delete account. Your account and online data are removed straight away; progress on your device stays.

Can I turn off sound or vibration? Yes, in Settings.
