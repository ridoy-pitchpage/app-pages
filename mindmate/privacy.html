MindMate

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

MindMate is a wellbeing companion for mood check-ins, guided breathing and a private journal. It is built so that what you write stays yours: MindMate does not collect any data about you.

What stays on your device

Your name (only the first name you type, to greet you), your check-ins, journal entries, breathing sessions, settings and optional passcode are saved only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time in the app: tap your initial on the Today screen, then Delete all data.

Backups

If you choose Save a backup, MindMate creates a file with your data and opens the standard iOS share sheet, so you decide where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop). We never receive it. Anyone with the file can read it, so keep it somewhere private.

Face ID and passcode

If you turn on the passcode, it is stored only on your device. If you turn on Face ID, iOS checks your face itself; MindMate only learns whether the check succeeded and never has access to your face data.

Reminders

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. It involves no server and sends nothing anywhere.

Children

MindMate is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, since it collects nothing at all.

Not a medical service

MindMate supports everyday wellbeing. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. If you are in danger or thinking about ending your life, call your local emergency number now, or find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Because MindMate collects no data, a change could never apply to data already collected.
