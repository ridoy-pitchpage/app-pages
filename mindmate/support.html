Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Tap Sign up, type a first name and you are in. Everything stays on your device.

How do I move my journal to a new phone?
On the old phone: tap your initial on the Today screen → Save a backup, and save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop it. On the new phone: open MindMate → Log in → Restore from a backup.

I forgot my passcode.
For your privacy the passcode can't be recovered. If you saved a backup, delete and reinstall MindMate, then choose Log in → Restore from a backup. Restoring opens the app; in Settings, turn the passcode off and on again to choose a new one. Without a backup, reinstalling starts fresh.

How do I turn on Face ID?
Set a passcode first, then turn on Unlock with Face ID in Settings (tap your initial on the Today screen).

How do I get a daily reminder?
Settings → Daily check-in reminder, then choose a time. You'll get at most one gentle reminder a day, and none on days you've already checked in.

What is the Feed?
A short set of cards made fresh each morning on your phone: something you wrote a week, a month or a year ago, your week as weather, gentle patterns from your check-ins, a one-minute practice, a kind word and a prompt. Tap the bookmark on any card to keep it in Kept. Nothing in it comes from the internet.

How do letters to future me work?
In the Feed, tap Write a letter, write a few lines and choose when it opens (in a week, a month, three months, or on a date). It stays sealed on your phone until that morning, then arrives in your Feed. If notifications are allowed for reminders, you'll also get a quiet note that day. Letters are included in backups.

Can I use light or dark mode?
Settings → Appearance: System follows your phone, or choose Light or Dark.

How do I delete my data?
Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app.
