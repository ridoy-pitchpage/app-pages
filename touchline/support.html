Common questions

When do the daily puzzles change?
At midnight, your local time. Everyone plays the same five puzzles each day; Arcade and Guess the player are unlimited.

How do streaks work?
Finish at least one daily puzzle a day to keep your day streak. Each daily game also keeps its own win streak, shown in My club.

A player's club or shirt number looks out of date.
Touchline uses a snapshot of the 2025–26 season, not a live feed. Tell us which player and we'll check it for the next update.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No. Everything works offline and your progress stays on your device.

How do I start over?
My club → Reset all progress. This can't be undone.

Where do the player photos come from?
From Wikimedia Commons, under free licences. The photographer and licence of each one are listed in My club → Photo credits.
