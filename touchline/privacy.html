Touchline

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Touchline does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the iPhone and iPad app sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your daily puzzles, streaks, stats, records, level and settings (such as sound effects) are saved only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time in the app: My club → Reset all progress.

Sharing a result

When you tap Share result after a game, Touchline hands a short, spoiler-free text (squares and a score) to the standard iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes. We never receive it.

Links to photo sources

Player photos are bundled inside the app. The Photo credits page links to each photo's page on Wikimedia Commons; tapping one opens it in your browser, where Wikimedia's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Touchline is a general-audience game. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, since it collects nothing at all.

Changes

If a future version adds an online feature, such as shared rankings, this policy will be updated first, with a new date, and will say exactly what is sent and why.
